Surveys AI Platform

Surveys AI Platform runs personality and beliefs surveys, built under the Ciao! umbrella.

The main survey runs 181 short prompts, one at a time. Statements such as "I rarely worry" or "I take charge", each rated on a six point accuracy scale, with progress tracked while you answer.

Answers feed a dashboard of trait scores. Every score is placed against the range where most people fall, so you can tell whether a 42 on Openness is genuinely high or merely ordinary. Three frameworks are covered: NEO, HEXACO and MPQ.

The last step is conversation. Once your scores exist you can ask Ciao! about them: what low Extraversion looks like in practice, how two traits interact, what moved since the previous run. The code is public on GitHub.
